# Factory Recovery Payment — 2026-09-11

**Status:** Completed and confirmed on Cardano Mainnet

## Purpose

Fund the agreed recovery of the legacy SUGR Factory and the handover of the remaining Factory SUGR recovery reserve to the SUGR Community Revival Team.

The payment was made to OrcaLabs (`$orcalabs`) under the agreed recovery arrangement.

## Payment Terms

The agreed **300 USDM** payment consists of:

- **200 USDM** — outstanding Factory hosting/server costs required to restore access
- **100 USDM** — workaround and handover work for transferring the remaining Factory SUGR recovery reserve to the Community Treasury

After the handover, the SUGR Community Revival Team will assume responsibility for legacy-holder verification, settlements, payouts and future recovery support.
