## Context

The legacy Factory was retired rather than reopened for public withdrawals.

Under the agreed recovery arrangement, OrcaLabs completed the workaround required to recover the Factory SUGR reserve and transferred the recovered assets to the Community Treasury.

Following the handover, the SUGR Community Revival Team assumes responsibility for legacy-holder verification, settlements, payouts, transaction fees and future recovery support.

The legacy Factory website has been placed into recovery/retirement mode while the remaining legacy-system close-out and website transition are coordinated separately.
