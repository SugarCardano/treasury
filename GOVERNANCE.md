# SUGR Treasury Governance

The SUGR Community Revival Treasury exists to support the recovery, maintenance and development of SUGR.

## Control

The treasury is controlled by a **2-of-3 Cardano native-script multisig**.

Authorized treasury signers:

- Nicholas
- IQ
- Akinga

**Two signatures are required to move treasury funds. No single signer controls the treasury.**

## Transparency

Material treasury movements will be publicly documented.

A treasury transaction record should normally include:

- purpose
- asset and amount
- Cardano transaction ID
- destination or counterparty where appropriate
- related pricing or team involvement where relevant
- supporting evidence where applicable

The Cardano blockchain is the authoritative record of **what moved**.  
The public treasury records explain **why it moved**.

## Related-Party Transactions

Where appropriate, material transactions involving core team members will have their relevant terms or pricing publicly disclosed before execution.

## Legacy Recovery Funds

Assets reserved for verified legacy-holder recovery are treated as **recovery reserves** and are not considered freely available operating treasury funds.

Legacy-holder recovery rules and records will be published separately when the recovery process is operational.

## Changes

Material changes to these treasury rules will be documented publicly.
